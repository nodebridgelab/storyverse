How to pick your next story by mood

2026-10-25

Feeling light? Try something casual and funny. Missing someone? Romance will keep you company. Carrying something heavy? A gentle story about loss and healing can help it settle.

If you do not know what you want, ask the story guide. Tell it how you feel in a few words and it will suggest three stories to start with.
