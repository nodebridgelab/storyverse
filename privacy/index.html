Who we are

STORYVERSE is a reading and listening site for original stories. Contact: nodebridgelabs@gmail.com.

What we store today

Your reading progress, saved books, text size, voice settings and 18+ confirmation are saved in your own browser (local storage). That data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

If accounts are added

When sign-in is enabled we will store your email address, your library and your reading progress so they follow you between devices. Payments, if offered, are handled by a payment provider; we never see your card details.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is shared with Google when a page opens. Read-aloud uses voices on your device or browser; some browsers send the text to their own speech service. If the optional AI story guide is connected to an AI service, the message you type is sent to that service to produce suggestions, so do not type personal information. Google AdSense serves ads on this site (see Cookies below). Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

Cookies

We show ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where the law allows, and they may collect information such as your IP address and the pages you visit. You can manage or turn off personalised ads at https://adssettings.google.com and learn how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Visitors in regions that require consent will be asked before personalised ads are used. We do not set any other advertising or tracking cookies of our own.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by emailing nodebridgelabs@gmail.com. We follow applicable laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where relevant, the GDPR.

Children

The Mature section is for adults aged 18 and over. The rest of the site is intended for general audiences, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
