Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

STORYVERSE ("we", "us", or "our") is operated by NodeBridge Labs.
Contact: nodebridgelabs@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use storyverse-reads.netlify.app (and any future custom domain that points to this site).

1. Information We Store

  • Reading progress, saved books, text size preference, voice settings, and 18+ confirmation are saved only in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it completely.
  • We do not create user accounts at this time. If we add accounts in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before collecting email addresses or other personal data.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which means your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads.
  • Text-to-speech (Listen feature): Uses your device or browser's built-in voices. Some browsers may send the text to their own speech service.
  • Optional AI Story Guide: If you use the Story Guide and it is connected to an external AI service, the message you type is sent to that service. Please do not type personal information.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads (including personalised ads where allowed by law). They may collect information such as your IP address and the pages you visit.
  • Hosting: Our hosting provider (Netlify) keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) for security and performance.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense. You can learn more about how Google uses data here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can manage ad personalisation at:
https://adssettings.google.com

You can also opt out of interest-based advertising via the Network Advertising Initiative:
http://www.networkadvertising.org/choices/

4. Children's Privacy

STORYVERSE is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Security

Because almost all user data stays on your device, the main risks are limited to your own browser. We take reasonable steps to keep the site itself secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we currently store almost nothing on our servers, most requests can be handled simply by clearing your browser data. For any other requests, email nodebridgelabs@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
