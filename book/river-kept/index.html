What the River Kept

by Meera Pillai · Sad & Healing · 3 chapters · Completed

After her father's death, Lakshmi returns to a Kerala village to scatter his ashes and finds the river has been keeping things for her.

Read nowOpen in the app

Start reading

Lakshmi carried her father in a brass urn on her lap, because the auto-rickshaw's seat had no safe place to put him.

The road to Kuttanad was a green argument between water and land. Banana leaves bowed at the windows. At a tea shop the owner asked, kindly, how many were travelling, and she said 'two' before she could stop herself.
