Letters from a Silent City

by Dev Kapoor · Mystery & Adventure · 3 chapters · Ongoing

In 1946 Shimla, a postal clerk inherits a sack of undelivered letters, all addressed to a street that burned down in 1932.

Read nowOpen in the app

Start reading

The sack was found behind the pigeonholes on a Tuesday, which was the day Habibullah had come to associate with discovery, disappointment, or both.

It was leather, officer's-issue, stamped with a registry mark three reorganisations out of date. Inside: one hundred and forty-one envelopes, none franked, all addressed in the same careful, backward-sloping hand to lanes and chowks the new municipal map had politely erased.
