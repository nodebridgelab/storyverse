Aarav Khanna had two numbers memorised: 14 and 90. Fourteen employees. Ninety days of cash.

His start-up, Ledgerly, rented half a floor above a dentist in Gurugram. On quiet nights you could hear the drill through the carpet, and Aarav had begun to feel that the building itself was trying to tell him something about root canals and runways.
