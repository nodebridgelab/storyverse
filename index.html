From the library

The Tuesday Tiffin Club: Five strangers on the fourth floor of a Pune software office swap lunch boxes every Tuesday. By the third week they are swapping secrets too.

Monsoon at Platform Nine: Two commuters, one delayed local train, and a shared umbrella that neither of them can afford to return.

The Sixth Floor Gamble: A bootstrapped founder has ninety days of cash, one impossible client, and a board that wants a miracle by quarter-end.

What the River Kept: After her father's death, Lakshmi returns to a Kerala village to scatter his ashes and finds the river has been keeping things for her.

The Last Lighthouse of Kanyakumari: A marine archaeologist returns to a decommissioned lighthouse to learn why three ships vanished in its final year of operation.

Letters from a Silent City: In 1946 Shimla, a postal clerk inherits a sack of undelivered letters, all addressed to a street that burned down in 1932.
