About STORYVERSE

STORYVERSE is a free reading and listening site for original short stories and short novels. We publish stories in five moods: Casual, Romance, Business, Sad Healing, and Mystery Adventure.

Every story is written to be read in short sittings — perfect for a phone, a quiet evening, or listening while you do something else. You can change the text size, save stories to your personal library (saved only on your device), and listen with a mood-matched voice.

Browse all stories

What you will find here

Each story is published in short chapters so it fits into a commute, a lunch break, or the last ten minutes before sleep. The library covers five distinct moods:

CasualWarm, everyday stories about neighbours, tiffin boxes, chai stalls, and the small kindnesses that make up an ordinary week.
RomanceQuiet love stories — a shared umbrella, a note left in a library book, two people who keep almost missing the same train.
BusinessStories from offices and start-ups: the nervous pitch, the impossible client, the colleague who surprises you, and the choice between money and meaning.
Sad & HealingStories that sit with grief, change, and the people we carry with us. Honest and unhurried, written for the moments when you need to feel less alone.
Mystery & AdventureShort mysteries set in Indian towns, forts, ferries, and hill stations. Puzzle-driven and atmospheric, with endings that are surprising but fair.

Every category has its own colour theme, so the reading experience shifts with the mood. A built-in Story Guide can suggest what to read based on how you feel.

How to read on STORYVERSE

  • No account needed. Open any story and start reading. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser.
  • Listen while you read. Press the play button on any chapter to hear it read aloud in a voice matched to the story's mood.
  • Save to your library. Tap the bookmark icon to save a story. Your library stays in your browser — private and instant.
  • Adjust text size. Use the text size controls on any story page to make reading comfortable on any screen.
  • Read on any device. The site works on phones, tablets, and computers with no app to install.

Who runs STORYVERSE

STORYVERSE is created and maintained by NodeBridge Labs, a small independent team that builds tools and publishes original fiction for readers who enjoy quiet, character-driven stories.

We believe good stories should be easy to find, easy to read, and free to enjoy. The site carries ads to support its costs, but we keep the reading experience as clean and uncluttered as we can.
