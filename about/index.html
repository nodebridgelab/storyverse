About STORYVERSE

STORYVERSE is a small reading and listening site for original stories in every mood: casual, romance, business, sad and healing, and mystery and adventure. You can read on any device, change the text size, save stories to a library on your own device, and press play to have a story read aloud in a voice that suits its mood.

What you will find here

Each story is published in short chapters so it is easy to read on a phone in a few minutes. Every category has its own colour theme, so the page feels different when you move from a gentle everyday story to a night-time mystery. A story guide can suggest what to read next if you tell it how you feel.

Who runs it

STORYVERSE is run by NodeBridge Labs, an independent team that publishes original fiction and builds small tools for readers. Stories marked as samples are examples while the library grows. Authors keep the rights to their work.
